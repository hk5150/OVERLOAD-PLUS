Do you take every set to failure?
The research says you might not need to.

✔ Meta-analysis of 15 studies: failure vs stopping short → only a trivial difference in muscle growth
✔ But don't stop too far short. The closer to failure, the more growth tends to increase
✔ The latest ACSM position stand lists 2–3 reps in reserve as one target
