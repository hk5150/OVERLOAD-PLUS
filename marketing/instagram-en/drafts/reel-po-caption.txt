Same weight, same reps, every week? That might be why you've stalled.
Here's what "progressive overload" actually means.

A little heavier. A little more. Gradually increasing the challenge on your muscles is the core principle of lifting.

✔ Adding reps counts too (similar muscle and strength gains to adding load / trained lifters, 8 weeks)
✔ More weekly sets → muscle growth tends to increase
✔ No need to add weight every session. To keep progressing, add a little somewhere

The key: know what you did last time. If you don't remember it, you can't beat it.
