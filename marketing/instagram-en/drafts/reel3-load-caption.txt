Light weights can't build muscle?
A meta-analysis of 21 studies says: it depends.

✔ ≤60% of 1RM, every set to failure → similar muscle growth (8.3% vs 7.0%)
✔ But heavier loads built more max strength (35.3% vs 28.0% / strength data mostly from untrained lifters)
✔ Want size? Lighter works if you get close to failure. Want strength? Go heavy too
