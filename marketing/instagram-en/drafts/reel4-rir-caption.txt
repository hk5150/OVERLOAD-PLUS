Can you explain "RIR"?
It's the number that makes your training log smarter.

RIR = Reps In Reserve (how many more reps you could have done)
• 10 reps, 2 more in the tank → RIR 2
• Couldn't do one more → RIR 0

✔ Muscle growth tends to increase closer to failure (the authors call it exploratory)
✔ The latest ACSM position stand lists RIR 2–3 as one target
✔ Same 155 lb × 10, but RIR 3 today vs RIR 2 last time? You're progressing
