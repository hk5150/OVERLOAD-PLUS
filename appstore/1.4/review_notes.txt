No account or login is needed; no demo credentials or sample files are required. The app includes an Apple Watch app (see HEALTHKIT below); the Watch app requires the iPhone app and is launched from it. Version 1.4 adds AI Coach, which copies a request to the clipboard and reads back an answer the user pastes in; it adds no new data handling or external services.

PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE: KURABELL is a strength-training log for teenagers and adults who lift at a gym and want to progress over time. It shows the previous session's weight, reps and RIR (reps in reserve) right next to every set being logged, so the user can see where to push today. It does not decide the next weight for the user: AI Coach only lists the plan the user pasted from their own AI chat, and nothing changes until the user taps "Use this plan".

HOW TO USE: Log tab > pick an exercise > enter weight and reps > tap an RIR value (a set counts as done when RIR is entered; the rest timer starts automatically) > "Save workout". History tab: calendar, charts and personal bests. Split tab (optional): choose a split; the next day's menu is prepared from the last session of that day. Settings tab: kg/lb, English/Japanese, body weight, "Connect to Apple Health" (off by default), backup/restore, Purchase.

IN-APP PURCHASE: one non-consumable, "Full Unlock" (com.hajime5150.kurabellplus.unlock). The first 10 saved workouts are free with every feature. Saving the 11th workout shows the paywall with the Full Unlock, which removes the save limit; nothing else is locked, and earlier workouts, charts and backups stay available without buying. It can also be bought in Settings > Purchase > "Unlock full version for (price)"; "Restore purchase" is in the same section.

EXTERNAL SERVICES: no server, analytics, ads or account system. Apple StoreKit (purchase); Apple HealthKit only if the user turns it on (on-device only). Two optional actions open websites in the user's browser: the "Form" button in each exercise card's row of actions (searches the exercise name on YouTube) and "AI Coach" (and "Ask AI" on the split and history screens), which copies the log to the clipboard and opens the AI chat site chosen in Settings; nothing is put in the URL and the app sends nothing. The AI's answer is pasted back in by the user and read on the device only. Unless one of these is tapped, the app makes no network requests apart from StoreKit.

HEALTHKIT (off by default): when the user turns on "Connect to Apple Health" in the Settings tab, the app asks for permission and then (1) saves each workout the user logs as Traditional Strength Training with its start and end times only (no calories), (2) reads the latest body weight to calculate bodyweight exercises, and (3) saves the body weight the user enters in Settings. If the companion Apple Watch app is installed, starting a workout on iPhone launches the watch app with HKHealthStore.startWatchApp and starts a strength-training HKWorkoutSession; the watch reads heart rate and active energy and saves that workout (with heart rate and active energy) to Health, and the iPhone does not write a second copy. Because the watch app is launched in the background and cannot show the Health permission sheet, the iPhone app asks for the watch app's permissions (heart rate and active energy) on its behalf, only when the watch app is installed; the iPhone app itself does not read or write them. Deleting a workout in the app also deletes the workout written to Health. Health data stays on the device; it is never sent to a server or used for advertising.

REGIONS: the app works the same in all regions (English and Japanese UI, kg or lb).
